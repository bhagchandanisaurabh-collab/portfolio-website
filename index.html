Hi, I’m Saurabh.

I’m a Geo-Information Scientist interested in understanding places, environments, and the systems that connect them. I work with geospatial data to explore real-world patterns and turn complex spatial information into insights that are easier to understand and use.

My work spans GIS, Earth Observation, remote sensing, spatial analysis, and WebGIS. I’ve worked on topics ranging from urban environments and climate-related challenges to spatial data platforms and interactive geospatial applications, combining research with practical problem-solving.

I enjoy working at the intersection of geospatial science, technology, and decision-making, whether that means analysing satellite data, building an interactive map, creating a spatial model, or exploring new applications such as digital twins. I’m always interested in learning new approaches and applying geospatial thinking to different kinds of problems.
